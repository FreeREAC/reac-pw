## Evidence honesty — what will and won't link a real desk

- **HOLD (steady-state) is captured ground truth** — the heartbeat, the
  600-frame link-check, the frame geometry/checksum are byte-verified against
  20 000+ real frames. The FSM's HOLD half is validated offline.
- **JOIN (cold-connect) is reconstructed**, not byte-verified. The grant-burst
  `cdea 04 03` is absent from every steady-state capture (it was only ever in the
  lost `/tmp/hs1.pcap` / `/tmp/dgs.pcap`, surviving as the §13b/§13d transcription).
  So a real M-5000 linking on the first try is **not guaranteed** — the JOIN
  builders are flagged experimental.
